# Obecolle2 v1.8 - Town Loop Rebuild

Goal: bring back the immediacy and visible rewards that made Obecolle1 fun.

- Town movement speed increased from 4.2 to 6.2.
- Five treasure chests appear around town each local day; walking near them collects them automatically.
- Treasure collection gives immediate sound/voice feedback and coins.
- Added eight town decorations sold in the shop. Buying one permanently changes the town view.
- Town decoration ownership is stored in specialItems so v1.7 saves remain compatible.
- Shop adds a dedicated town-decoration tab and moves to a denser 4x2 card layout.
- Global panels/buttons/dialogs/HUD were compacted to reduce oversized empty frames.
- Friends can now receive furniture, clothes, hats, and glasses.
- Gifted clothes/accessories visibly change the friend both in town and in their room.
- Gifted furniture continues to auto-arrange in the friend's room.
- Clothing artwork itself remains provisional and is intentionally deferred for the later Codex visual pass.
- Typing excavation and v1.7 room flow are preserved.
